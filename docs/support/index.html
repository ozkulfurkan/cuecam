Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/ozkulfurkan/cuecam/issues. Please include your phone model, OS version and the app version (Settings → About).

Frequently asked questions

Where are my videos?

In your photo gallery, in the "Prompter" album, and in the app's Recordings tab.

The text is too fast or too small.

Use the speed and size sliders on the recording screen, or set defaults in Settings. Tap the text to pause; hold the screen to scroll twice as fast.

Can I move the text closer to the camera?

Yes. Before recording, drag the text box and resize it with the handle in its corner.

The app says camera or microphone access is off.

Tap "Open Settings" and turn on Camera and Microphone for Cue Cam, then return to the app.

A recording was not saved.

Allow Photos access for Cue Cam in Settings. If storage was full or the phone overheated, the recording stops by itself and the part recorded so far is kept.

Can I use a Bluetooth remote or keyboard?

Yes: space or Enter pauses and continues, the arrow keys change speed and jump through the text.
