Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

Cue Cam ("the app") is a teleprompter camera app for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: your scripts and your videos never leave your phone unless you share them yourself. The app has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Scripts you write, paste or import, and your settings (speed, text size, video quality, language, text box position) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Videos and audio you record are saved to your device's photo library, in an album called "Prompter". The developer never receives them.
  • When you use Share, the video is handed to the app you choose through your phone's share sheet. What happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
CameraTo film you while you read your script.
MicrophoneTo record your voice with the video.
Photos / mediaTo save recordings to the "Prompter" album and list them in the Recordings tab.

Camera and microphone are used only while the recording screen is open. You can turn any permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

Crash and performance reports

To find and fix bugs, the app sends crash reports and limited performance data to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), which processes them on our behalf. A report contains technical information such as the error and where it happened in the code, the app version, the device model, the operating system version, free memory and storage, and a random installation identifier used to count crash-free sessions.

Reports do not contain your scripts, videos, audio, screenshots, name, email address or contacts. The app tells Sentry not to store IP addresses. Reports are not used for advertising, are not sold, and are not linked to your identity. Sentry keeps them for up to 90 days.

Legal basis (GDPR / KVKK): our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable and working.

What we do not do

  • No account, login or personal profile.
  • No advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking across apps and websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties for their own purposes.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting a script in the app removes it. Uninstalling the app removes all scripts and settings. Videos stay in your photo library until you delete them there. Crash reports cannot be tied to you, so they cannot be looked up individually; they are deleted automatically after the retention period.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or KVKK in Türkiye) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, or to object to its processing. Because the app does not collect data that identifies you, there is usually nothing stored about you; you can still contact us with any request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
